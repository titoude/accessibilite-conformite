# Third-party notices for the paired pilot

The toolkit's [MIT license](LICENSE) does not replace the licenses of upstream
benchmark materials. This page identifies the sources used in the paired pilot.

## W3C ACT fixtures

The fixtures under `benchmarks/paired-pilot/calibration/` originate from the
[W3C ACT Rules test cases](https://www.w3.org/WAI/content-assets/wcag-act-rules/testcases.json).
Their source URLs and content hashes are recorded in that directory.
The [W3C notice and license](benchmarks/paired-pilot/calibration/LICENSE-W3C.txt)
is preserved with the fixtures and applies to that material.
